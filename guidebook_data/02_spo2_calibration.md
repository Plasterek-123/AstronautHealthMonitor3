# SpO2 Measurement & Sensor Calibration

## Reference Ranges
* **95% - 100%:** Normal
* **91% - 94%:** Warning (check cabin gas mix and repeat measurement)
* **Below 90%:** Critical Alert (initiate oxygen protocol in ICE chapter)

## Procedure
1. Warm up the hand before placing the optical clip.
2. Place the sensor on the index or middle finger.
3. Shield the sensor from direct cabin lighting.
