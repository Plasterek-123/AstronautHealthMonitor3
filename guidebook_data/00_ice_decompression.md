# Critical Decompression Protocol

## Immediate Actions
1. **DON OXYGEN MASK:** Pull emergency mask from the nearest bulkhead unit within 5 seconds.
2. **VERIFY SUIT SEAL:** Check helmet locks and glove pressure integrity.
3. **SOUND ALARM:** Broadcast **CODE RED** to mission control and adjacent modules.
4. **EVACUATE AREA:** Seal hatch to the compromised sector if structural failure is confirmed.
