# Anaphylactic Shock Protocol

## Immediate Actions
1. **ADMINISTER EPINEPHRINE:** Use auto-injector (EpiPen 0.3mg) into the lateral thigh muscle.
2. **AIRWAY MANAGEMENT:** Ensure clear airway, administer high-flow oxygen.
3. **MONITOR VITALS:** Watch SpO2 and blood pressure closely in telemetry monitor.
