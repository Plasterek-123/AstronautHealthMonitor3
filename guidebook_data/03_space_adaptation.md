# Space Adaptation Syndrome (SAS)

## Overview
Caused by sensory conflict between the visual system and the vestibular apparatus in weightlessness.

## Countermeasures
1. Avoid rapid head movements during the first 72 hours of flight.
2. Maintain visual orientation cues inside the module.
3. Keep hydration above **2.0 L/day**.
