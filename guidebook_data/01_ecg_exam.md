# 3-Lead ECG Procedure in Microgravity

## Required Equipment
* Telemetry unit **BioMonitor-X**
* 3 disposable gel electrodes
* 70% isopropyl alcohol swabs

## Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Skin Prep:** Clean the chest sites with an alcohol swab and let dry for 30 seconds.
2. **Electrode Placement:**
   * **Red (RA):** Right infraclavicular fossa.
   * **Yellow (LA):** Left infraclavicular fossa.
   * **Green (LL):** Left lower costal margin.
3. **Stabilization:** Have the astronaut hold a handrail and relax shoulder muscles.
