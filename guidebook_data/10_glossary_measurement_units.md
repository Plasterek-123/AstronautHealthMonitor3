# Measurement units quick reference

BPM: heart beats per minute. mmHg: blood pressure. °C: temperature. Gy: absorbed energy per unit mass. Sv: weighted radiation-dose quantity. Always keep the unit and measurement period with the value. µ means one millionth; m means one thousandth.
