# System Medical Glossary

* **BPM (Beats Per Minute):** Heart rate frequency. Normal resting range: **60-100 BPM**.
* **SpO2:** Estimated arterial blood oxygen saturation.
* **ECG:** Electrocardiogram recording cardiac electrical activity (P-QRS-T waves).
* **Actigraphy:** Non-invasive monitoring of rest and activity cycles.
* **Dosimeter:** Device measuring ionizing radiation exposure rate (µSv/h).
