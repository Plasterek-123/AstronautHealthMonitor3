# Severe Trauma & Hemorrhage Protocol

## Action Plan
1. **LOCATE BLEEDING:** Identify primary hemorrhage site.
2. **TOURNIQUET APPLICATION:** Apply windlass tourniquet proximal to the wound (high and tight on limb).
3. **HEMOSTATIC GAUZE:** Pack deep wounds with Celox/QuikClot gauze and apply direct pressure for 3 minutes.
