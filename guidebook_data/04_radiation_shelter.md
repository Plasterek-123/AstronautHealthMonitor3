# Solar Particle Event (SPE) Shelter Protocol

## Action Checklist
1. Verify real-time dosimeter rate in the **Monitoring** tab.
2. Relocate crew to the central shielded zone (surrounded by water storage bags).
3. Avoid viewport areas and thin hull sections until the alert clears.
