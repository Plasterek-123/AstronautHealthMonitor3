# Mineral intake references

US adult DRI examples (nonpregnant, nonlactating). Not a mission prescription. Reviewed 2026-11-14.

RDA = recommended dietary allowance; AI = adequate intake; UL = tolerable upper intake level; CDRR = chronic disease risk reduction intake. These are separate concepts. Below target is not a diagnosis; unlogged or missing intake is not zero intake.

Base table: ages 31–50. Age adjustments: calcium target 1200 mg for women 51+ and everyone 71+, calcium UL 2000 mg at 51+; magnesium targets 400/310 mg for men/women 19–30; iron target 8 mg for women 51+; phosphorus UL 3000 mg at 71+; chromium AI 30/20 µg at 51+; chloride AI 2000 mg at 51–70 and 1800 mg at 71+.

Corrections to supplied table: copper 900 µg = 0.9 mg (not 900 mg); iron UL 45 mg (8 mg is a male intake target); phosphorus UL 4000 mg through age 70 (not 1.5 × calcium intake); magnesium UL 350 mg applies to supplements/medications only; chloride UL 3600 mg; fluoride UL 10 mg; molybdenum UL 2000 µg. Sodium 2300 mg is CDRR, not a toxicity UL. US selenium UL 400 µg is used consistently with this US reference set; other jurisdictions can differ.

Daily checks use recorded intake only. Food catalog values are fictional demonstration data and contain only a subset of minerals. Missing composition stays incomplete. Individual mineral logs use canonical units after conversion. Magnesium upper-limit checks count supplements separately.
